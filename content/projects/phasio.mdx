## Problem

Manufacturing buyers often needed technical knowledge to select the right material, process, dimensions, and fulfilment setup.

## Insight

A guided conversation could make these decisions easier when grounded in the manufacturer’s own knowledge and storefront configuration.

## Execution

I led ManufacturingGPT, native GST support, and the Flywire integration. I also introduced PostHog funnel analytics, targeted Crisp resolutions, and forty Playwright tests across critical workflows.

## Impact

The three launches collectively contributed to **20 new B2B deals**. Funnel drop-off fell by **24%**, support issues fell by **80%**, and automated tests saved **eight engineering hours per sprint**.
