## Problem

A waitlist of more than 900,000 people put pressure on an AI video product's ability to provide access, deliver useful capabilities, and respond to issues as it grew.

## Insight

Scaling access and improving the product experience had to move together. Prioritization needed to connect user needs, AI quality benchmarks, and commercial goals.

## Execution

As founding Product Manager, I led AI engineering delivery, wrote PRDs, established benchmarks and KPIs, and used competitor research to prioritize work. We shipped six core features and four follow-up releases, alongside changes to pricing, credits, and packaging.

## Impact

- Scaled product access from **10k to 80k users per week**. This measures access provision, not weekly active users.
- Releases supported ARR growth from **$4.2M to $6M in three months** as part of the wider team's work.
- Higher-tier adoption grew from **10% to 40%**.
- Reduced major disruption duration from **24–48 hours to under six hours**.

These results are drawn from my résumé; internal research and product documents are not published here.
