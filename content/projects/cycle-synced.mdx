## Problem

I wanted a workout experience that took my cycle into account, without adding another account or complicated setup.

## Insight

A small, immediately usable prototype was a useful way to explore the idea and share it with others.

## Execution

Built and launched a personal prototype in about one hour, combining cycle-aware workout suggestions, creatine recommendations, and YouTube suggestions.

## Impact

A live, publicly shared prototype. No adoption or health-outcome claims are made for this personal experiment.
