## Problem

Growing a B2B marketing SaaS product meant connecting acquisition experiments with the features and automations customers actually used.

## Insight

Product queries, experiments, and pilot feedback provided complementary inputs for prioritization.

## Execution

Worked on ten features, analyzed more than 200 queries, and ran more than 20 growth experiments. Launched LinkedIn and Warpcast automations and supported eight B2B pilots.

## Impact

The résumé reports conversion growth of 8%, CTR growth of 5%, and more than 100 signups. LinkedIn and Warpcast automation adoption reached 79% and 20%, respectively. Eight B2B pilots converted to six-month paid engagements.
