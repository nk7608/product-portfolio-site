## Problem

Premises verification can add friction to a loan journey when customers are unsure what evidence to provide or struggle with capture, permissions, and connectivity.

## Insight

Evidence needs to be current, relevant, associated with the place, and usable. More photos do not automatically mean better verification.

## Execution

Designed a guided journey through consent, readiness, capture, review, and outcome. The concept uses minimal physical proof, accommodates salaried and self-employed contexts, and provides camera, location, and network recovery paths.

The rollout proposal moves from shadow evaluation to automatic passing and then scaling, with additional checks where evidence is weak.

## Impact

This is a **design concept**. Completion, first-attempt success, time, and risk-operations load are proposed evaluation metrics. Manual-review targets in the deck are aspirations, not achieved outcomes.
