## Problem

An AI agent for DevOps needs to fit the working reality of enterprise SRE teams: CI/CD processes, security requirements, and compliance constraints.

## Insight

The product direction needed evidence from the people doing the work. Discovery and validation sessions provided a way to test assumptions before committing to delivery.

## Execution

At Progress, I worked across **34 discovery and validation sessions**, tested ten hypotheses, and analyzed eight agent competitors. I translated the resulting direction into **eight PRDs and 36 epics**.

In parallel, I explored three go-to-market channels and supported conversations with prospective users.

## Impact

- Validated **eight of ten hypotheses**.
- Moved from **MVP to demo-ready in four months**.
- Converted conversations with **ten prospective users into three pilots**.
- Built a qualified waitlist of **40 people** across three channels.
