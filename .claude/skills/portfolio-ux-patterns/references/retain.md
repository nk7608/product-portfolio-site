# What to retain

## Story and evidence
- The career arc: Junior PM (Phasio) → Growth PM (Blaze) → APM (Progress/Opsmith) → Founding PM (Vidrush), newest first, with duration per role.
- Persona self-selection (hiring / building / curious) — now the first-visit intro + "Arranged for … Change".
- Open-to-work status with location, remote/hybrid and Europe & US hours, in the hero.
- Three headline outcomes with the note that they're team outcomes; metric definitions inside each role.
- Honesty notes on experiments ("Independent proposal; not commissioned by Swish", etc.).
- Recommendations with name, title and relationship; writing links; real artefacts (prototypes, PDFs, code).
- Personal details (Fix You, books, Bangalore pitstops, Scope Runner) — the human texture the references have.

## Product
- Product cover scenes (`cover-scenes.tsx`), flat and brand-tinted, inside opened rows and case pages.
- `/work/[slug]` long-form pages and `/experiments` for sharing and SEO.
- Company logos (local, verified) as small marks beside role names.

## Quality floor
- Pre-paint preferences (no flash), reduced-motion respect, 2px accent focus rings, native dialogs, semantic headings, `aria-expanded` on rows and sections.
- CSP + security headers in `next.config.ts`, sandboxed game iframe, YouTube via nocookie only after Play, pinned dependencies.
- Tests in `tests/` (prefs, lens, content, résumé PDF, service worker) and the measure/contrast scripts.
